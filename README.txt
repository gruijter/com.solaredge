Follow your SolarEdge installation live in Homey: solar production, home consumption, the grid, your battery and EV charging. Use the data in Flows and in Homey Energy.

Two ways to connect:
- Cloud: sign in with the e-mail address and password of your mySolarEdge account. Works on every Homey, including Homey Cloud.
- Modbus: connect directly to the inverter on your local network. Works only on Homey Pro. Modbus TCP must be enabled on the inverter (your installer can do this in SetApp).

Cloud devices: inverter, meter, battery, EV charger and Home Load Controller.
Modbus devices: inverter, meter, battery and SolarEdge Home EV Charger, including control of output power, grid export, battery charging and EV charging.

Known limitations: the cloud login does not work for accounts that use two-factor authentication (2FA) or sign in with a company account (single sign-on).
